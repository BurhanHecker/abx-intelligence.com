Cookie Policy

The short version: this website does not use cookies, and there is nothing here for you to consent to.

Last updated: 6 September 2026

The short version

This website sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. It stores nothing in your browser and it loads no files from any other company's servers.

That is why you are not being shown a cookie banner. There is genuinely nothing to consent to.

What cookies are

A cookie is a small file a website asks your browser to store, so it can recognise your browser later. Cookies are often used to keep you logged in, remember preferences, measure how many people visit a site, or follow people between sites to target advertising.

What we use instead

Nothing. We built this site so that it does not need any of it:

  • No cookies. None, including the "strictly necessary" kind.
  • No browser storage. We use no local storage, session storage, or similar.
  • No analytics. We do not count or profile visitors. We have no dashboard of your behaviour.
  • No advertising or tracking pixels.
  • No embedded third-party content. Fonts, styles, scripts, and graphics are all served from our own domain, so browsing this site does not disclose your visit to anyone else.

You can verify this yourself. Open your browser's developer tools, look at the Application or Storage tab for cookies, and the Network tab for requests to other domains. Both should be empty.

The one exception

If you fill in and submit the contact form, the details you typed are sent to Web3Forms, which delivers them to our inbox. That happens only when you press the button, never from simply reading the site, and it involves no cookies. This is explained in our Privacy Policy.

Our web server's logs

Like almost every web server, the machine hosting this site may keep routine technical logs of requests, which can include IP addresses. These are generated by our hosting provider for security and reliability, not by cookies, and we do not use them to build any profile of you.

To complete before launch: confirm with your hosting provider what its server logs record and how long they are kept, then state that here.

Controlling cookies generally

Although this site sets none, you can control cookies for every site from your browser's settings, usually under Privacy. Browsers let you block cookies, delete existing ones, or clear them automatically when you close the window.

If this ever changes

If we later add anything that uses cookies, for example analytics, we will update this page first and put a proper consent mechanism in place beforehand. We will not start tracking visitors and mention it afterwards.
