Services

What we deliver.

This list covers what we build most often. It is not the limit. Most projects combine several of these, delivered as one system.

Software and systems

  1. Custom software

    Systems built from the ground up for one school and one problem.

  2. School management systems

    The records and workflows a school runs on, structured the way that school works.

  3. Student and staff portals

    One place for notices, tools and sign-ups, with a view for each role.

  4. Databases

    Reliable records that stay accurate and are easy to find.

Events and automation

  1. Event and registration systems

    Sign-ups, capacity, schedules and check-in in one place.

  2. Workflow automation

    Multi-step routines that run themselves instead of by hand.

  3. Digital forms and approvals

    Structured submissions with routing, tracking and a full history.

  4. Integrations

    Connecting the tools you already use so nothing is typed in twice.

AI and data

  1. AI-assisted marking

    Suggested marks and feedback against your criteria, with teachers deciding.

  2. Dashboards

    Clear views of attendance, results and requests as they happen.

  3. Search and summaries

    Finding and condensing information across your records.

  4. Communication

    Notices, emails and reminders that reach the right people.

Design and care

  1. Interface design

    Screens designed for the people who use them every day, on the devices they carry.

  2. Hosting

    Monitored, secure hosting set up for reliability.

  3. Maintenance

    Updates, fixes and security patches that keep the system healthy.

  4. Support

    A direct line to the people who built the system.

Not on the list

If it runs on paper or spreadsheets, ask.

The most useful projects often start as a problem nobody has named yet.

If a process at your school depends on paper, email chains, spreadsheets or one person's memory, it can almost certainly be built properly. And if changing the process would help more than software would, we will say so.
