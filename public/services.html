Services

What we deliver.

This list covers what we build most often. It is not the limit. Most projects combine several of these, delivered as one system.

Software and systems

  • Custom softwareSystems built from the ground up for one school and one problem.
  • School management systemsThe records and workflows a school runs on, structured the way that school works.
  • Student and staff portalsOne place for notices, tools and sign-ups, with a view for each role.
  • DatabasesReliable records that stay accurate and are easy to find.

Events and automation

  • Event and registration systemsSign-ups, capacity, schedules and check-in in one place.
  • Workflow automationMulti-step routines that run themselves instead of by hand.
  • Digital forms and approvalsStructured submissions with routing, tracking and a full history.
  • IntegrationsConnecting the tools you already use so nothing is typed in twice.

AI and data

  • AI-assisted markingSuggested marks and feedback against your criteria, with teachers deciding.
  • DashboardsClear views of attendance, results and requests as they happen.
  • Search and summariesFinding and condensing information across your records.
  • CommunicationNotices, emails and reminders that reach the right people.

Design and care

  • Interface designScreens designed for the people who use them every day, on the devices they carry.
  • HostingMonitored, secure hosting set up for reliability.
  • MaintenanceUpdates, fixes and security patches that keep the system healthy.
  • SupportA direct line to the people who built the system.
Not on the list

If it runs on paper or spreadsheets, ask.

The most useful projects often start as a problem nobody has named yet.

If a process at your school depends on paper, email chains, spreadsheets or one person's memory, it can almost certainly be built properly. And if changing the process would help more than software would, we will say so.
