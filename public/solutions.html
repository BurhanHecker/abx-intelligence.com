Solutions

What we build, and where it is in use.

Every system starts from how a school already works. These are the four kinds we build most often, each with a real example.

01 · Portals

School portals

One place for students and one for staff, sharing the same data. Notices, sign-ups and selections that used to live on paper and in inboxes move into a system everyone can see.

  • Separate views for students, staff and administrators
  • Notices, memos and calendars
  • Selections and sign-ups with live capacity
  • Exports for the office

In use: student and staff portals at ABA Oman International School, including Clubs and CAS Excursion Week.

02 · Events and registration

Event and registration systems

Sign-in, programme, choices and check-in for conferences and school events, with an organiser dashboard that shows what is happening as it happens.

  • Passwordless sign-in with a code sent by email
  • Workshop choices that help organisers size rooms
  • Check-in by NFC tap, QR code or at the desk
  • Live attendance and exports for organisers

In use: ISCO 2026. 814 delegates registered, 709 checked in, 2,413 workshop picks.

03 · Automation

Process automation

Requests that route themselves, records that stay in step, and reminders that go out on time. The steps that never needed a person stop taking up one.

  • Digital forms with routing and approvals
  • Scheduled and triggered jobs
  • Email notifications and reminders
  • Data kept in sync between systems
  • A record of who changed what, and when

In use: ISCO check-in writes straight to the live attendance list; CAS excursion places update for every student the moment one changes.

04 · AI tools

AI-assisted tools

AI used where it saves real time, with a person in charge of the result. Our marking system suggests marks and feedback; teachers decide.

  • Marking written answers against the teacher's criteria
  • Feedback and follow-up practice for each student
  • Results by question, so teachers see what to reteach
  • Every mark can be changed by a teacher

In use: the assessment system at ABA Oman International School.
