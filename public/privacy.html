Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what we do not do with it.

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Who we are

ABX Intelligence builds custom software for schools and organizations. It was founded by Burhan Dairkee and Advaith Prem and is based in Muscat, Oman.

ABX Intelligence is operated by Neo Technology Solutions LLC (NeoTecx), a company registered in the Sultanate of Oman under commercial registration number 1355566. Invoices for our work are issued under that company, and payments are made to it.

You can reach us at sales@abx-intelligence.com.

Summary

This website does not track you. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from any other company. The only personal information we receive through this site is what you deliberately type into the contact form and send us.

What this policy covers

This policy covers personal information we handle as the party deciding why and how it is used, which in practice means enquiries sent through this website.

It does not cover personal information held inside systems we build for a client organization. In that situation the client organization decides how the data is used and we act on its instructions. The client organization's own privacy notice governs that data, and our obligations are set out in our written agreement with them.

Information we collect

Through the contact form we collect only:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your organization, if you choose to give it (this field is optional)
  • The subject category you select
  • The message you write

We ask for nothing else. We do not collect your phone number, address, job title, or any identity or payment information through this website. Please do not include sensitive personal information, or personal information about students, in your message.

Why we use it, and on what basis

We use these details for one purpose: to read and reply to your enquiry, and to keep a record of the exchange. We rely on the consent you give by ticking the box on the contact form. You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing us, and we will delete the enquiry.

We do not add you to a mailing list. We do not send marketing. We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose your details for anyone else's commercial purposes.

Who receives your information

Three service providers are involved:

  • Cloudflare hosts this website and delivers its pages. It does not receive what you type into the contact form.
  • Web3Forms receives what you type into the contact form and delivers it to our inbox. It keeps a copy for 30 days, then deletes it.
  • Hostinger provides our email. Enquiries, and anything you email us directly, are stored in our mailbox there.

Nobody else receives your details. If you would rather not use a form service, you can email us directly instead.

Transfers outside Oman

These providers operate outside Oman. Web3Forms stores submissions on servers in the United States. Cloudflare delivers this website from a network of data centres around the world. Hostinger stores our email on servers outside Oman.

Personal information you send us may therefore be transferred outside Oman. We use only established providers that encrypt data in transit, and we keep what they hold to the minimum described in this policy. Oman's Personal Data Protection Law places conditions on transferring personal data abroad, and we aim to meet them.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence only as long as it is useful for the conversation it belongs to, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you get back in touch. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we will. The copy held by Web3Forms is deleted automatically after 30 days.

Security

This website is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection, and contact form submissions travel over an encrypted connection to our form provider. We restrict access to the inboxes and accounts that receive enquiry data to the two founders.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise. If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we will act on our obligations under Omani law, including notifying the regulator and affected individuals where required.

Children's information

This website is aimed at school and organization staff, not at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it.

Systems we build for schools may hold information about students. Where that is the case, the school is responsible for deciding what is collected and for obtaining any consent required, including from parents or guardians. We handle that data only on the school's documented instructions, and we treat it with the additional care its sensitivity demands.

Your rights

Under Oman's Personal Data Protection Law you have rights over your personal information. In broad terms these include the right to be told what we hold, to obtain a copy, to have inaccurate information corrected, to have information deleted, to withdraw consent you previously gave, and to object to certain uses.

To exercise any of these, email us and we will respond as promptly as we reasonably can. We will not charge you for making a request. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Ministry of Transport, Communications and Information Technology.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will say so clearly rather than quietly editing the text.

Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints about privacy can be sent to sales@abx-intelligence.com.
