About

Two founders, building software for schools.

ABX Intelligence was founded by Burhan Dairkee and Advaith Prem, both 16 and in Grade 11 at ABA Oman International School in Muscat.

How it started

We started from the inside.

A lot of school software is designed by people who have never spent a day in a school. The result is systems that technically work, and staff who quietly work around them.

We saw the same processes break down again and again at our own school: forms that had to be printed, sign-ups on paper sheets, information kept in three places that never agreed. So we built the systems to fix them, and they are in use today.

Being students is why we understand the people our software is for. It is not an excuse for lower standards. We work with clear scope, written agreements, and a commitment to look after everything we deploy.
