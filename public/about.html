About ABX

Two Founders. One Vision.

ABX Intelligence was founded by students who experienced the inefficiencies of existing systems firsthand and decided to build better solutions themselves.

The origin

We Started From the Inside.

Most software for schools is designed by people who have never lived a school day. The result is familiar: systems that technically work, and workflows that quietly route around them.

ABX Intelligence began differently. We saw the same processes break down repeatedly. Forms that had to be printed. Requests that lived in inboxes. Information that existed in three places and agreed in none. So we built the systems that fixed them. Those systems are in use today.

That perspective is a structural advantage, not a footnote. We understand the users of school software from the inside: how information actually moves, where processes break down, and what people work around every day. We build with that knowledge, and we hold ourselves to professional standards: clear scope, written agreements, and a long-term commitment to everything we deploy.
