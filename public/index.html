ABA Oman International School · Portals

Student and staff portals

One place for the school tasks that used to run on paper forms and sign-up sheets. Students choose their clubs and their CAS excursion, and see places open and close live as others choose. Staff work from the same data: the student memo, a rotation calendar with each teacher's timetable, and an overview of every selection.

  • One excursion per student, switchable instantly
  • Live spaces instead of a first-come paper list
  • Staff view with exports and a live board
ABA Oman International School · Assessment

AI-assisted marking

Teachers build a test and students take it in the browser. Written answers are marked against the teacher's criteria, and each student is emailed their results with practice questions aimed at what they got wrong.

  • Teachers can change any mark
  • Students can ask for a review of a question
  • Tests can be shared across a department

Screens show sample data. No real student, staff or delegate information appears on this site. ISCO figures come from the app's own records.

How we work

From the first conversation to years of use.

  1. 1

    Understand

    We start with the people doing the work and map what happens today, step by step.

  2. 2

    Design

    We design screens around real moments in the school day and check them with the people who will use them.

  3. 3

    Build

    We build in short steps you can try as we go, and test on the phones and laptops your people actually use.

  4. 4

    Look after it

    After launch we fix, update and extend the system for as long as you use it.
