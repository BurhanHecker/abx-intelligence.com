Privacy Policy

How ABX Intelligence handles information collected through this website and the systems we build.

Overview

ABX Intelligence is committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who visits this website or uses a system we have built. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is handled. Last updated: 2026.

Information we collect

Through this website, we collect only the information you choose to submit through our contact form: your name, email address, organization, and the message you write. We do not sell, rent, or trade this information.

Within systems we build and operate for client organizations, data handling is governed by the agreement with that organization. In those cases ABX Intelligence acts as a processor on the organization's behalf.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries you send us
  • To provide, maintain, and support the systems we build
  • To improve the reliability and security of our services
  • To meet legal or contractual obligations

Data retention

Enquiry information is retained only as long as needed to respond to and record the enquiry. Data held within deployed systems is retained according to the retention terms agreed with the client organization.

Security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes access control, encryption in transit, monitored infrastructure, and routine backups.

Third-party services

This website uses a third-party form delivery service to route contact submissions to our inbox, and a third-party font service to load typefaces. These providers receive only the data necessary to perform those functions.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To do so, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable period.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@abx-intelligence.com.
